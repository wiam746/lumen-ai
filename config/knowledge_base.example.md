# Organization knowledge base (example)

Copy this file to `config/knowledge_base.md` (git-ignored) and describe your organization:
opening hours, services, procedures, FAQ. Lumen adds it to the system prompt.

Rules of thumb:
- Public, non-personal information only. Do not list individuals (names, phone numbers, e-mails).
- With an external provider, this text is sent with every request.
- Keep it under ~12,000 characters.

## Example

- Support desk: open Monday to Friday, 09:00-17:00. Requests go through the intranet portal.
- Official documents and results are published on the intranet, never shared through Lumen.
