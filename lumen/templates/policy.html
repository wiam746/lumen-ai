Acceptable use policy

Version {{ policy_version }}. By creating an account you agree to the rules below.

Do

  • Use Lumen for professional or educational tasks: explaining, summarising, drafting, brainstorming.
  • Check important answers against reliable sources or with a person.
  • Tell Lumen's reviewers if an answer is wrong or harmful, using Contest.

Do not

  • Enter personal data about yourself or others: names with contact details, ID or bank numbers, passwords, health or other sensitive information. Lumen rejects what it recognises, but it cannot recognise everything: the responsibility is yours.
  • Use Lumen to decide, rank, grade or sanction people. It informs; humans decide.
  • Ask for harmful, illegal, discriminatory or deceptive content, or try to bypass the safeguards.
  • Share your account, or attempt to access other users' data.
  • Present AI output as verified fact or as your own unreviewed work where disclosure is required.

What happens if rules are broken

Flagged messages are reviewed by a person who may validate, warn, delete the content, or suspend the account, always with a written reason. You can contest every decision, and a suspended account can still contest, export and delete its data.
