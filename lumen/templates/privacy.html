Privacy notice

Version {{ policy_version }}. This notice explains what Lumen does with your data (GDPR Art. 13; Moroccan Law 09-08, Art. 5).

Who is responsible

The data controller is {{ org_name }}. Contact for privacy questions and to exercise your rights: {{ dpo_contact }}.

What we collect, and why

  • Account: a username (pseudonym), a password hash, your role, consent record and sign-in time. Purpose: authentication and access control.
  • Requests: the messages you send and the answers you receive, stored encrypted. Purpose: provide the assistant and your personal history.
  • Audit trail: pseudonymous user ID, action, time and a truncated IP address. Never your message content or username. Purpose: security, accountability and investigating misuse.

We do not collect your name, e-mail, device identifiers, or analytics. We load no third-party fonts, scripts or trackers.

Legal basis

Provision of an internal service in the organization's mission (public interest or legitimate interest, GDPR Art. 6(1)(e)/(f)); security logging is a legitimate interest. No sensitive data is intended to be processed: the system rejects identifiers and flags sensitive topics.

Who receives your messages

{% if provider.external %}

This deployment sends your messages to {{ provider.label }}, operating on {{ provider.where }}. This is a transfer outside the organization and outside the country. It is covered by a documented assessment and safeguards. You may object in Settings: Lumen then answers with the built-in offline assistant.

{% else %}

This deployment processes your messages on {{ provider.where }}. No message is sent to a third party. If an administrator enables an external provider, this notice and the Ask page will say so before your next message.

{% endif %}

Reviewers (administrators) can read a message only if it was flagged as possibly sensitive, and each such access is recorded.

How long we keep it

  • Requests: {{ retention_days }} days, then deleted automatically (or sooner if you delete them).
  • Contests and warnings: 12 months after the decision.
  • Audit trail: 12 months. Failed sign-in counters: 24 hours.

Your rights

Access and export, rectification, erasure, objection, and not being subject to a decision based solely on automated processing: all are available from My data and Settings. Lumen takes no decision with legal or similar effect on you: a human decides every moderation action, and you can contest any of them. We answer within one month.

You may lodge a complaint with a data protection authority: in Morocco, the CNDP (Commission Nationale de contrôle de la protection des Données à caractère Personnel); in the EU, your national supervisory authority.

Security

Messages are encrypted at rest, passwords are hashed with scrypt, sessions use protected cookies, sign-in attempts are throttled, and administrative actions are written to a tamper-evident audit trail.
