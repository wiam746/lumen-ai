How this AI works

Lumen is an AI system. This page explains in plain terms what it is, what it can and cannot do, and who is accountable.

What it is

Lumen sends your message to a language model and shows the reply. The model predicts plausible text; it does not look facts up unless an administrator has given it organization documents, and it does not understand or verify what it writes.

Currently active: {{ provider.label }} on {{ provider.where }}.

Known limits and risks

  • Errors and invented facts. Answers can be confidently wrong, including sources and quotations.
  • Bias. Models reflect biases of their training data and may treat groups unequally.
  • Over-reliance. A fluent answer is not a verified one. Check what matters.
  • Privacy. The personal-data filter uses patterns; it misses names, indirect identifiers and disguised data.
  • Manipulation. Text pasted into Lumen can try to steer the model (prompt injection).

Safeguards

  • Identifiers and secrets are rejected before storage or processing.
  • Every answer is labelled as AI-generated; it is never presented as a decision.
  • Sensitive topics are flagged for human review. A person, never the AI, decides about warnings, deletions and suspensions.
  • Decisions leave a tamper-evident audit trail and can be contested.
  • Data is encrypted, deleted automatically, and under your control.
